The Story in Brief

The Three-Body Problem immerses readers in a future where humanity is on the brink of first contact with an alien civilization. Following the Cultural Revolution in China, a brilliant physicist named Ye Wenjie makes a fateful decision that sets in motion a series of events with far-reaching consequences. As the world grapples with the implications of this contact, the story delves into the complex philosophical and scientific questions surrounding the Trisolaran civilization and their enigmatic Three-Body Problem, which defies the predictability of classical physics.
