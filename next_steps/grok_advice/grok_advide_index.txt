3. Monetization Strategy (Diversified & Compliant)
Primary Goal: Move beyond sole reliance on Amazon.
Recommended Mix:

Amazon Associates — Convenience, Kindle deals, Prime shipping.
Bookshop.org — 10% commission + supports indie bookstores (ethical appeal).
Audible — High payouts ($10–15 per signup or sale) – great for “listen while commuting” angle.
Other options: Barnes & Noble, Kobo, Apple Books, streaming services (digital rentals).

Implementation:

Create a clean “Where to Buy / Experience” section on every page with multiple links + safe wording.
Use neutral phrases: “View on Amazon (often discounted on Kindle)”, “Buy on Bookshop.org (supports independent bookstores)”.
Always include clear disclosures.

Avoid:

Any “X% off with my link” language (Amazon ban risk).
Hard price comparisons that go stale.
