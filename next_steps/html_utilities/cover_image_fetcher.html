📚 Book Cover Image Fetcher

Paste your CSV (image_filename, asin, affiliate_link) or just ASINs with filenames. The tool will attempt to fetch each cover from Amazon's image CDN and let you download them with the correct filename.

Accepts: full CSV rows  |  ASIN|filename.jpg  |  bare ASINs (filename auto-generated)

wget Script (run in your images/ folder)

If browser fetching is blocked by CORS, copy this script and run it in your terminal inside your images/ directory.
