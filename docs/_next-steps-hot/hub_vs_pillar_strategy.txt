1. CORE ANSWER
1.1 Yes — building hubs is more important than creating multiple Lonesome Dove pillar pages.
1.2 Multiple pillar pages for the same entity will dilute authority and cause keyword cannibalization.
1.3 The correct strategy is: one pillar page per entity + supporting hubs across broader intent clusters.

2. WHY MULTIPLE PILLAR PAGES FOR LONESOME DOVE IS A MISTAKE
2.1 Entity conflict: Google expects a single authoritative page for a single entity.
2.2 Keyword cannibalization: multiple pages compete against each other instead of reinforcing one ranking signal.
2.3 Diluted authority: backlinks, internal links, and engagement signals are split across pages.
2.4 Ranking ceiling effect: multiple weak pages tend to stay in positions 20–80 instead of one reaching top 10.

3. WHAT GOOGLE ACTUALLY WANTS
3.1 ONE canonical Lonesome Dove page:
3.1.1 Covers book vs miniseries
3.1.2 Covers synopsis
3.1.3 Covers timeline and setting
3.1.4 Covers character and adaptation differences
3.1.5 Covers FAQs (true story, genre, release context)
3.2 Supporting structure around it (hubs + internal links)

4. WHY HUBS ARE MORE IMPORTANT RIGHT NOW
4.1 Your dataset shows many overlapping intents across multiple titles.
4.2 Google is already clustering these intents, but your site is not structurally aligned.
4.3 Hubs solve:
4.3.1 Internal linking logic
4.3.2 Topical authority distribution
4.3.3 Crawl efficiency
4.3.4 Ranking consolidation across many pages
4.4 At your current stage, structure > content volume.
