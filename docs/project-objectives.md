BooksVersusMovies.com — Project Objectives
==========================================

WHAT THIS SITE IS
-----------------
BooksVersusMovies.com publishes comparison pages for books and their film or
television adaptations. Each page answers three questions a reader actually
has: which version is better, what are the meaningful differences, and should
I read the book before watching.

The site exists at the intersection of two things: a genuine editorial interest
in how stories change when they move between media, and a scalable affiliate
revenue model built on Amazon book purchases. The writing should be good enough
that the site is worth reading. The structure should be optimised enough that
it ranks, gets clicked, and converts.

The goal is not to be a listicle factory. It is to be the smartest, most
opinionated comparison resource on the internet for this specific niche —
the kind of site you would bookmark and return to, written with the
specificity and confidence of someone who has actually read the books and
watched the films.


REVENUE MODEL
-------------
Primary: Amazon Associates affiliate commissions on book purchases
  - Affiliate links on every page via buy button and book cover image
  - CTAs placed at decision moments: after quick answer, after recommendation,
    after verdict
  - Target: reader who has just decided the book is worth reading clicks
    through and buys

Secondary (future consideration): film/streaming affiliate links where available


CONTENT PHILOSOPHY
------------------
Voice
  Precise, opinionated, no hedging. Write like a knowledgeable critic, not a
  book report. Strong verdicts. Specific references to characters, scenes,
  actors, and techniques. No "both versions have their merits" without
  immediately saying which merits matter more.

Structure
  Structured for search, written for humans. The template handles SEO mechanics
  — quick answer, comparison table, FAQ — so the writing doesn't have to carry
  that weight. The writing lives in the difference sections and the verdict.

Tone
  The Atlantic-style criticism inside an SEO-optimised shell. Not BuzzFeed.
  Not an academic essay. A smart friend with taste who has read the book and
  watched the film and will tell you which to do first.

What to avoid
  - Hedging verdicts to avoid taking a position
  - Generic observations that apply to any adaptation ("the book has more detail")
  - Padding differences with plot summary
  - Listicle language and manufactured urgency


SEO AND CONVERSION TARGETS
---------------------------
Primary search intents this site targets:
  - "[title] book vs movie"
  - "should I read [title] before watching"
  - "differences between [title] book and movie"
  - "is [title] movie faithful to the book"
  - "[title] which is better book or movie"

Funnel targets (current baseline, from GSC):
  - CTR: currently ~1.3%, target 3-6% after revision pipeline
  - Impressions to first sale: currently ~5,000+, target ~1,200 after optimisation

Ranking priority:
  - Pages currently sitting at position 5-12 are the highest ROI targets
  - Push these to top 5 before worrying about new content


CURRENT SCALE
-------------
  ~170 live review pages
  Coverage: literary fiction, thriller, YA, romance, sci-fi, memoir,
            historical fiction, horror, fantasy


PIPELINE OBJECTIVE
------------------
The pipeline exists to make the site's performance predictable and scalable.

Revision pipeline
  Takes existing pages and brings them up to the full structural spec:
  quick-answer block, optimised CTAs, verified FAQ coverage. Goal is to
  unlock value from pages already ranking but not converting.

Greenfield pipeline
  Produces new pages that are born fully optimised — no revision pass needed.
  Goal is to scale coverage without scaling manual effort.

Both pipelines share one renderer, ensuring every page on the site has
identical structure regardless of how it was produced.


SUCCESS METRICS
---------------
Short term (4 weeks post-deployment)
  - CTR improvement on revised pages vs pre-revision baseline
  - First affiliate sale

Medium term (3 months)
  - Pages per month ranking in top 5 for primary keyword
  - Revenue per 1,000 impressions

Long term
  - Direct traffic and return visitors (indicator of brand, not just SEO)
  - Expanding coverage to 300+ titles via greenfield pipeline
