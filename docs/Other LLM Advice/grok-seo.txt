My Prioritized Go-Forward Fix List (High / Medium / Low Impact)
Here’s my refined, site-specific version. I put SEO-heavy items higher because they directly impact your GSC data (CTR on existing impressions, positions around 10–13).
High Impact (Do First – Quickest Wins for Traffic & UX)

Homepage Hero & Intro Rewrite
Add a clear, benefit-driven hero (as in the welcome page draft I gave you). Explain what the site is, the promise (“honest differences + spoilers + verdict”), show stats, and have a strong CTA to “Browse All” or featured pages.
Add a “Start Here / Featured” Section
Highlight 4–6 of your strongest/timely pages (Verity, Reminders of Him, A Beautiful Mind, Fault in Our Stars, etc.) with verdict badges, short hooks, and links. This reduces clutter and guides visitors to high-quality content.
Optimize Titles, Metas & On-Page on Existing Pages
Continue the automation we started: emotional + keyword-rich titles (“…Book vs Movie: Shocking Changes Hollywood Hid”), updated intros, “What the Book Does Better / Movie Does Better” sections, and consistent verdicts near the top. Focus first on pages with impressions but low CTR.
Make Verdict System Prominent & Consistent
Ensure every comparison page and homepage cards clearly show “Book Wins”, “Film Wins”, or “Too Close to Call” early.

Medium Impact (Next 1–2 Weeks)
5. Improve Navigation & Internal Linking
Add a clean top menu (Home, Browse All, Genres, About). On each article, add a “Related Comparisons” section with 6–8 relevant links.

Reduce Homepage Clutter
Shorten the main list or move most of it to /index_alt.html. Keep the homepage more curated.
Create or Strengthen an About Page
Explain your approach (“honest, spoiler-heavy, no sugarcoating”), method for verdicts, and why readers can trust the opinions.

Lower Impact (Later / Ongoing)
8. Add Browsing Paths / Collection Pages (e.g., “Best Book-to-Movie Wins”, “Most Surprising Changes”, “Colleen Hoover Adaptations”) — these build topical authority but aren’t urgent yet.
9. Advanced SEO (schema enhancements, faster mobile, more FAQ optimization, freshness signals via occasional updates).
Simple Rule to Follow (My Version)
If a visitor lands on any page, they should immediately understand: “This site gives honest book vs movie breakdowns with spoilers and tells me what to do first.” And they should know exactly what to click next.
One-Line Priority Statement
Optimize what you already have (titles, homepage experience, internal links) to turn existing impressions into clicks and better signals — then keep adding curated depth.
The Perplexity list is a good starting point, but your site is stronger than a pure “list site,” so lean into your detailed articles as the core strength while making the homepage a better gateway.
