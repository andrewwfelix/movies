# Job Description — Content Researcher & Data Preparer
BooksVersusMovies.com — Part Time / Freelance
==========================================


## About the Site

BooksVersusMovies.com publishes honest, opinionated comparisons of books and
their film or TV adaptations. Every page picks a winner and explains why.
The site has 180+ published comparisons covering literary fiction, thriller,
sci-fi, romance, and more — and is growing fast.

We're a small operation building something genuinely useful for readers and
film lovers. No corporate fluff. No hedged verdicts. Just honest criticism
and affiliate-supported content.


## The Role

We're looking for a detail-oriented researcher to help us scale our content
pipeline. This is a part-time freelance role — roughly 5-10 hours per week
to start, with potential to grow.

You'll be the person who keeps the content pipeline moving: finding the data
we need to publish new pages, sourcing images, and doing quality checks on
published content. No writing required. No coding required.

If you love books and films, care about getting details right, and can follow
a clear process without needing hand-holding, this role is for you.


## What You'll Do

Each week you'll work through a clear task list — the specifics will vary
as the site grows, but the work generally falls into a few categories.

Most of your time will be spent on content data preparation: finding and
verifying information needed to publish new pages, sourcing images, and
making sure existing content is complete and accurate. You'll work from
structured checklists and templates — nothing is ambiguous or left to
interpretation.

Some weeks will include light research tasks: tracking down new book-to-film
adaptation announcements, verifying release dates and cast information, or
checking that recently published pages look correct in a browser. These tasks
are well-defined and come with clear acceptance criteria — you'll always know
exactly what "done" looks like.

You won't write content, touch code, or manage anything that requires access
to the site's technical infrastructure. Your work feeds into a pipeline that
the site owner runs. Think of yourself as the person who makes sure the
inputs are right so the outputs are clean.


## What We're Looking For

**Must have:**
- Strong attention to detail — you notice when something is wrong
- Comfortable with basic web research (Amazon, YouTube, Google)
- Able to follow written instructions precisely
- Reliable — you do what you say you'll do, when you said you'd do it
- Genuine interest in books, films, or both

**Nice to have:**
- Familiarity with book publishing or film industry
- Experience with affiliate marketing or Amazon Associates
- Experience doing SEO-related tasks (GSC, indexing, etc.)
- Comfort with JSON files (we'll train you — it's just filling in fields)

**Not required:**
- Writing or editorial experience
- Technical or coding skills
- Social media experience


## Compensation

Hourly rate, paid weekly via PayPal or Venmo.
Rate: $15-20/hour depending on experience.
Starting commitment: 5-10 hours/week.
Fully remote. Flexible hours — work when it suits you, hit your weekly targets.
