Here are the highest-leverage ways to grow traffic to BooksVersusMovies.com, with an emphasis on what fits your niche best.

SEO topics to target
Build content clusters around one strong pillar page, then publish supporting pages for related queries such as “Should I read the book first?”, “Is the movie faithful?”, “Book vs movie ranking,” and “best adaptation of [genre]”. That helps search engines understand the site as a focused authority instead of a loose collection of comparisons. Pages that answer specific questions also have a better chance of winning featured snippets and long-tail search traffic.

Content that earns clicks
Create pages that are useful even before a reader knows the title, such as “best book-to-movie adaptations,” “most faithful movie adaptations,” and “biggest changes from book to film”. Those broader evergreen pieces can attract more search traffic than only title-specific comparisons. Then internally link those evergreen posts to your individual comparison pages so traffic spreads through the site.

Social channels to use
Short-form video is probably the best promo format for your niche, especially on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels. You can turn each comparison into a 20–40 second “book wins or movie wins?” clip, which gives people a quick opinion hook and a reason to click. Pinterest can also work well because adaptation content is visually searchable and tends to perform well for evergreen topics.

Link building ideas
Get other book, movie, and pop-culture creators to reference your verdicts by making your pages highly quotable and easy to cite. Add a simple embed or share card for each comparison so bloggers and newsletter writers can link back to the exact page they discuss. Collaborations with book reviewers, film bloggers, and podcast hosts can also bring referral traffic and backlinks at the same time.

Email and return visits
Start an email list with a weekly “book vs movie roundup” so you can bring readers back without depending entirely on search traffic. Include one new comparison, one trending adaptation, and one “fan favorite” from the archive each week. Reintroducing your best-performing older content is a proven way to get more mileage out of what already works.

Practical next steps
Publish 5–10 evergreen hub posts around adaptation questions and rankings.

Add FAQ sections and richer summaries to every comparison page.

Turn each page into 2–3 short social clips and a Pinterest graphic.

Build a simple email signup with weekly or biweekly updates.

Reach out for guest mentions or backlinks from book and movie creators.
