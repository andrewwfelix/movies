What is a spotlight? Our spotlight pages go beyond a standard book vs. movie comparison. They cover the full picture — the source novel in depth, the adaptation choices, sequels and related works, historical context, and a definitive verdict on what to read, watch, and in what order. They take longer to read and longer to write. We only make them for adaptations that reward that level of attention.
